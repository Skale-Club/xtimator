<objective>
Wire the WhatsApp side of AGENT-01: the owner can ask the assistant to message a client, get an exact-echo draft back, and only on their NEXT distinct reply does anything actually send. This plan is pure channel binding — every actual decision (recipient resolution, rate limit, gate, dispatch) already lives in 178-02; this plan's job is turn-taking (draft turn vs. confirm turn) and never letting a bare "yes" reach the normal estimate-creation classifier.

Purpose: Closes Pitfall 8 (no confirmation-gate precedent — this uses a genuinely distinct WhatsApp round-trip, not the `manage-tools.ts` immediate-write pattern) for the WhatsApp channel specifically, and closes the routing gap where a pending send confirmation is invisible to `whatsapp_sessions` (it is NOT an estimate-draft session, so it needs its own early check in `handler.ts`, mirroring — not reusing — the existing `awaiting_confirm` branch).
Output: A draft-side MANAGE tool + a confirm-side pre-classification check, both provably never breaking the existing intent-router/handler test suites or the frozen QA-01 regression.
</objective>
