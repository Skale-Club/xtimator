## Decisions Made

- Company config and authorized sender are separate tables (D-11 authority boundary)
- Active-phone partial unique index enforces exactly one active sender globally
- Multi-phone companies classified as pending_review with explicit reason (never first-member-wins)
- normalizeWhatsAppPhone exported from account-registry module (no existing suitable helper)
- findActiveWhatsAppSender uses .limit(2) + length check instead of .maybeSingle() for ambiguity detection
- Logs only last-4-digits of phone numbers on zero/ambiguous results
