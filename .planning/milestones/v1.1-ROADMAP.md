# Milestone v1.1: Dark-first UX & Modern Redesign

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-04-22
**Phases:** 9 (Phase 9 only)
**Total Plans:** 8

## Overview

v1.1 modernises the product UX on three fronts: (1) dark mode becomes the system-wide default with a user-persisted toggle persisted to DB and respected cross-session/cross-device via SSR cookie hydration; (2) onboarding becomes a survey-style one-question-at-a-time flow with smooth transitions and progress indication; (3) the core UI elements (buttons, inputs, cards, tables, modals, empty states, overlays, navigation shells) get a full visual redesign for a modern, polished feel built on a shared design-token vocabulary. The public estimate view (`/estimate/*`) and generated PDFs stay on the light palette for recipient professionalism.
