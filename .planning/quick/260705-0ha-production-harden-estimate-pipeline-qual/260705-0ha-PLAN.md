<objective>
Production-harden the estimate pipeline in two additive, byte-identity-preserving ways:

1. WI-1 — make estimate-quality signals (server-vs-AI discrepancy magnitude, guardrail
   moves, price-research hit/miss telemetry, flagged-unpriced count) OBSERVABLE in
   production and alert on anomalies via the already-wired Sentry sink — WITHOUT changing
   any computed total and WITHOUT breaking the existing structured log line.
2. WI-2 — harden `computeEstimateTotals` against invalid inputs (negative/non-finite tax
   rate, deposit exceeding total) so the engine never emits negative tax or a negative
   balance — as a strict no-op for every currently-valid input.

Purpose: A 100-estimate / 10-tenant test batch surfaced three production blind spots —
3/100 estimates had a >20% silent server-vs-AI correction (max 45.3%) visible ONLY in
console.info; a 97% research evidence-gate rejection rate with no attempted-vs-usable
telemetry (a degraded provider is undetectable); and compute-totals has no guard against a
malformed tax rate or an over-total deposit (only the UI floors the latter today).

Output: a new pure quality-signal module + its Sentry reporter, an additive optional
telemetry field on ResearchOutcome, the generate-estimate wiring, defensive guards in
compute-totals, and two new unit-test files. No DB schema change; no persistence-path change.
</objective>
